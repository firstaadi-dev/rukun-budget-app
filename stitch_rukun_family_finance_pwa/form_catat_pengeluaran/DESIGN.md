## Brand & Style
The design system embodies a collaborative family financial companion built on empathy, trust, and shared accountability. It resolves the anxiety and taboo surrounding household money management by balancing mature financial structure with a welcoming, joyful, and approachable tone.

### Design Movement
**Organic Warm Minimalist with Soft Tactile Accents.** The visual style blends clean, structured data visualization with deeply human, tactile characteristics: generous rounded corners, warm cream canvases, cushioned pill containers, and breathable padding. It rejects cold, clinical fintech conventions in favor of a living-room atmosphere—orderly, lighthearted, yet utterly dependable.

### Emotional Target
- **Relief & Harmony:** Neutralizing financial confrontation between partners through shared visibility and mutual ownership.
- **Warmth & Trustworthiness:** Emitting the emotional safety of a shared home ledger rather than an institutional bank interface.
- **Delight in Discipline:** Encouraging small, consistent habits through rewarding visual feedback, gentle status indicators, and approachable microcopy.

## Colors
The palette balances natural grounded tones with crisp functional indicators to make household accounting clear and stress-free.

- **Primary (`#0D6E6E` - Forest Teal):** The foundational anchor. Used for primary navigation states, dominant action buttons, primary balance metrics, and structural header components. Expresses long-term stability and stewardship.
- **Secondary (`#10B981` - Crisp Mint) & Tint (`#E8F5F1` - Soft Sage):** Signals incoming capital, completed budget goals, positive cash flow, and collaborative nods. Always paired with deep teal or dark slate for text accessibility.
- **Expense / Outflow (`#FF6B6B` - Warm Coral) & Tint (`#FFF0F0` - Melon Mist):** Designates debits, expense allocations, and spent funds. It shifts away from aggressive crimson to reduce physiological stress while maintaining unambiguous distinction.
- **Warning / Nudge (`#F59E0B` - Citrus Amber) & Tint (`#FFFBEB` - Sun Mist):** Highlights nearing budget thresholds, pending partner confirmations, or billing reminders.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAFAF7` - Warm Cream) & Surface (`#FFFFFF` - Crisp Card White):** Pure white surface cards sit atop a warm, organic linen background, creating subtle contrast without visual fatigue.
- **Neutral Text Primary (`#1F2937`) & Secondary (`#6B7280`):** Balanced slate tones prevent harsh contrast over warm cream surfaces while maintaining strict WCAG AAA contrast ratios.

## Typography
The system employs a dual-font pairing optimized for warmth and mathematical precision:

1. **Plus Jakarta Sans:** Primary brand, heading, and body font. Its geometric structure, humanized terminals, and welcoming roundness set an approachable, family-friendly tone across all conversational microcopy and headings.
2. **Inter (Tabular Figures Enabled):** Reserved strictly for numeric ledgers, transaction records, currency amounts, percentages, and data tables (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`). This guarantees vertical alignment across multi-line expense listings and budget comparisons.

### Editorial Tone & Microcopy
Microcopy uses polite, informal-standard Indonesian (Bahasa Indonesia santun dan akrab). Rather than dry accounting terms like "Debet/Kredit," it uses natural household phrases:
- *Saldo Bersama* (Joint Balance)
- *Belanja Dapur* (Groceries)
- *Catat Pengeluaran* (Log Expense)
- *Capaian Tabungan* (Savings Progress)
- *Giliran kamu verifikasi bon ini, ya!* (Your turn to verify this receipt!)

## Layout & Spacing
The layout model is **Mobile-First PWA (Default viewport: 390px)**, expanding gracefully into multi-column tablet and desktop views.

### Viewport Adaptation & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (<640px):** Single-column stack inside a centered container (max-width `430px` on wider screens if viewed via desktop browser). 16px lateral margins, 80px bottom clearing for navigation dock and the prominent action button.
- **Tablet (640px – 1024px):** 2-column asymmetric grid (Overview & Quick Actions left; Feed & Shared Chat right). Margins increase to 32px.
- **Desktop (>1024px):** 3-column layout (Navigation & Accounts, Main Workspace, Partner Activity & Analytics) pinned inside a max `1200px` shell.

### Vertical Rhythm
Layout components use strict multiples of 4px/8px:
- Compact card padding: 16px (`space-md`).
- Hero summary cards: 20px to 24px (`space-lg`).
- Component interior element gaps: 8px (`space-sm`) between title and subtitle; 12px to 16px between data clusters.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is produced through **tonal layering and warm diffused ambient shadows**, steering clear of stark grey drops.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Flat `#FAFAF7` background.
- **Level 1 (Card Surfaces):** Crisp `#FFFFFF` card background with a 1px border using `rgba(13, 110, 110, 0.06)` and ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(13, 110, 110, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Modals, Sheets, Sticky Bars):** Elevated floating elements over a blurred scrim (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.3)` backdrop-filter: `blur(8px)`). Shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(13, 110, 110, 0.12)`.
- **Level 3 (Prominent Floating Primary Action):** The center *Catat* button utilizes a directional tinted glow: `0 8px 24px -2px rgba(13, 110, 110, 0.35)`.

## Shapes
A roundedness tier of `2` anchors the identity:
- **Small Badges & Avatars:** Fully circular/pill shapes (`rounded-full` / `9999px`).
- **Input Fields & Secondary Buttons:** `12px` to `16px` border-radius (`rounded-lg` to `rounded-xl`).
- **Core Cards & Overview Widgets:** `16px` to `20px` border-radius.
- **Bottom Navigation Dock & Action Sheets:** `24px` top/all-around radius to evoke an organic, pebble-like comfort under the thumbs.

## Components

### 1. Persistent 5-Tab Bottom Navigation & Floating "Catat" Button
- **Bar Container:** Floating dock pinned to the bottom screen safe area. Surface is `#FFFFFF` with 24px rounded top edges, border-top `1px solid rgba(13, 110, 110, 0.08)`, elevated at Level 2.
- **Tabs (5 items):**
  1. *Beranda* (Home)
  2. *Transaksi* (Transactions)
  3. *Catat* (Prominent Center Action)
  4. *Anggaran* (Budgets)
  5. *Lainnya* (More / Family Settings)
- **Center "Catat" Button:** Raised 56x56px circular button protruding 16px above the bar line. Filled with Forest Teal (`#0D6E6E`), housing a crisp white plus/pencil glyph. On tap, triggers a tactile spring animation and pops a bottom sheet for instant ledger logging.

### 2. Family Balance Hero Card
- Distinctive container styled with a delicate gradient from Forest Teal (`#0D6E6E`) to Deep Emerald (`#094E4E`), text set in crisp white.
- Displays combined household net worth alongside micro-badges representing each partner's avatar and individual input share.
- Quick summary pills inside the card: *Pemasukan Bulan Ini* (green tint badge) and *Pengeluaran* (coral tint badge).

### 3. Transaction List Items
- Clean tabular rows housed inside Level 1 white cards.
- Left: Category icon encased within a 44x44px soft-tinted circle (`#FFF0F0` for food/groceries, `#E8F5F1` for salaries/bonuses, `#FFFBEB` for utilities).
- Middle: Transaction title in Plus Jakarta Sans SemiBold (`body-md`), with a bottom subtitle denoting category, partner name avatar badge, and time.
- Right: Monetary amount in Inter SemiBold (`currency-md`), prepended with `+ Rp` (Mint) or `- Rp` (Coral).

### 4. Collaborative Budget Meter Cards
- White surface cards featuring a linear progress track (`h-3 bg-gray-100 rounded-full`).
- Bar fills with Forest Teal or Mint when within safety limits (<80%), dynamically transitions to Citrus Amber (80-99%), and Warm Coral once exceeded.
- Displays spending velocity alongside supportive microcopy (e.g., *"Aman! Sisa Rp 450.000 untuk 12 hari ke depan"*).

### 5. Input Fields & Form Elements
- Heights standardized to 48px for thumb comfort.
- Background set to neutral warm cream `#FAFAF7` with an inactive border of `1.5px solid rgba(13, 110, 110, 0.12)`.
- Focus state activates an outline of `2px solid #0D6E6E` with a subtle teal glow ring.
- Large numeric amount input for logging expenses defaults to 32px Inter tabular digits with automatic Indonesian thousand separators (`Rp 150.000`).

### 6. Interactive Filter Chips
- Height 36px, fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full`).
- Inactive: `#FFFFFF` border `1px solid #E5E7EB`, text `#4B5563`.
- Active: Background `#E8F5F1`, border `1.5px solid #0D6E6E`, text `#0D6E6E` font-bold.
